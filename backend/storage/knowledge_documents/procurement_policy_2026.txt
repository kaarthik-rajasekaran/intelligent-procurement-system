ENTERPRISE PROCUREMENT POLICY AND THRESHOLD GOVERNANCE

Section 1: Approval Matrix
1.1 Purchases under $5,000 require Department Supervisor approval.
1.2 Purchases between $5,000 and $50,000 require multi-vendor RFQ bidding with at least two qualified quotations.
1.3 Purchases exceeding $50,000 require Finance Director review.

Section 2: Vendor Selection Guidelines
2.1 The lowest quote is not automatically awarded. Evaluators must balance unit cost (40%), lead time (20%), delivery reliability (20%), and vendor quality (20%).
2.2 If an evaluator overrides the system-recommended vendor, a mandatory documented business rationale must be entered.
2.3 High-reliability vendors with certified quality badges should be favored for mission-critical IT infrastructure.

Section 3: Purchase Orders and Payment Terms
3.1 No procurement commitments may be made without an officially generated Purchase Order (PO).
3.2 PO generation is idempotent and requires prerequisite supervisor final approval.
3.3 Net 30 payment terms apply to all certified vendor fulfillment orders.
